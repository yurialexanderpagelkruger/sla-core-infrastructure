The scenario

The day the server goes down, the business stops

A business with everything centralized on a single old server, without reliable backups or real monitoring, is one failure away from losing days of billing.

Single point of failure

One machine handles email, files, database and billing. If it goes down, everything goes down.

Unverified backups

Copies that were never restored are not backups. They are a promise no one tested.

No visibility or alerts

No one knows something is failing until a customer reports it. Diagnosis arrives too late.

The solution

Infrastructure designed to keep running

Every layer is built, documented and tested. Nothing is left to chance.

01

Migration to Debian / RedHat

Services separated by function, RAID for disk tolerance, updated and reproducible base system.

02

Automated backups

Custom script + cron + generation rotation, with a scheduled real restore test.

03

Monitoring and alerts

Zabbix or Prometheus with thresholds, notifications and a real-time status dashboard.

04

VPN and hardening

Encrypted remote access, firewall, hardened SSH and a reduced attack surface.
